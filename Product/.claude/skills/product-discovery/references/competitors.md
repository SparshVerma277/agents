## Search constraints

- Anchor every search to a capability, not the brand alone —
  `"ChargeAfter" change order management`, not `ChargeAfter`.
- Max 5 subpages per competitor. Stop at the cap and report the cap.
- Public pages only. Skip anything paywalled, gated, or login-walled — and
  never work around a paywall.
- Marketing copy is a **claim**, not a shipped capability. Cite the URL and
  attribute it: "ChargeAfter's site states X" — not "ChargeAfter has X".
- Undated pages are undated. Do not infer recency, and do not present a
  competitor page as current unless it carries a date you can read.
